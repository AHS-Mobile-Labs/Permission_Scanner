PRIVACY POLICY - Permission Scanner
Last Updated: October 2026
Version: 1.0.4

1. OVERVIEW & PHILOSOPHY
Permission Scanner ("the App"), developed by AHS Mobile Labs, is an open-source, privacy-first Android application designed to help you inspect and understand app permissions on your device.

Our core commitment is absolute data privacy: Permission Scanner operates 100% on your device, entirely offline. We do not operate external servers, do not collect personal data, and do not track your activity.

2. DATA PROCESSING & ON-DEVICE OPERATION
All data analysis is performed locally on your device:
• Installed Apps & Metadata: Package names, app labels, declared permissions, install sources, SDK versions, and declared components are read via Android's PackageManager.
• APK Inspection: APK files selected for analysis are parsed locally to identify declared permissions, trackers, and risk indicators.
• Risk & Justification Scoring: Privacy scores, risk ratings, and capability justifications are calculated entirely on-device.
• Local Storage: Scan cache, fingerprints, and timeline history are stored locally in Hive storage within the app's sandboxed private directory.

No scan data, device identifiers, or app lists are ever transmitted to AHS Mobile Labs or any third-party server.

3. ANDROID PERMISSIONS USED
The App requests only permissions strictly necessary for its security audit functions:
• QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Required to enumerate installed applications and inspect their permissions.
• POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional permission used solely to deliver local alerts when high-risk permission patterns are detected.
• WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 and below): Used strictly when you choose to export audit reports to your device's storage.

4. NO THIRD-PARTY SHARING OR TRACKING
• Zero Telemetry: No analytics, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or cloud trackers are integrated into the App.
• No Data Sales: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer your data under any circumstances.
• Offline Guarantee: The App requires no network connection for scanning or analysis.

5. USER CONTROL & DATA DELETION
You retain complete ownership and control over your data:
• You can clear all cached scan data and history anytime via the "Clear Cache" button in the app.
• Uninstalling the App or clearing app data through Android Settings permanently removes all locally stored data.

6. SECURITY & LEGAL COMPLIANCE
• Sandboxed Storage: Stored data is protected by Android application sandboxing and device-level encryption.
• Regulations: By processing zero personal data and maintaining strict on-device isolation, the App complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and applicable global privacy standards.
• Children's Privacy: The App does not collect any personal information from children or any other users.
